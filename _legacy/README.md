# Getting Started

👨‍💻 Hilliard M. Scott III Portfolio Website

🌟 Overview and Purpose

This repository houses the source code for the personal portfolio website of Hilliard M. Scott III. The site serves as a comprehensive digital resume, showcasing a diverse professional background that spans engineering, entrepreneurship, athletics, and music.

The primary goal of the website is to position the author as a versatile and experienced Problem Solver by providing concrete examples of work and achievements across multiple disciplines, bridging the gap between hardware, software, business, and personal passions.

Core Sections:

What I Do (Services): Highlights the multifaceted professional roles—Engineer/Entrepreneur, Coach/Player, and Musician—demonstrating a wide range of skills.

Who I Am (About): Delves into the underlying problem-solving philosophy and the combination of skills (Hardware, Software, Entrepreneurial Spirit, Quality Control) that define the author's approach.

My Work: A curated portfolio of professional projects, from electrical engineering for NASA to co-founding successful technology ventures.

My Play: Showcases personal passions, including extensive hockey coaching experience and dedication as a bassist.

⚙️ Design and Structure

The portfolio is designed for simplicity, clarity, and direct navigation, ensuring visitors can quickly access information about the author's diverse experience.

2. Design Principles

a. Clarity and Readability

The site uses high-contrast text and a well-structured, easy-to-read layout. Sections are clearly delineated with prominent headings, making information scanning efficient.

b. Visual Storytelling

Each section is supported by relevant imagery (personal photos, project thumbnails) to make the content more engaging and to reinforce the narrative of the author's professional journey. The use of image cards for "My Work" and "My Play" organizes the portfolio items cleanly.

c. Responsive Navigation

A sticky navigation bar with clear links (Home, What I Do, Who I Am, My Work, My Play, Contact) ensures smooth navigation between the primary sections of the single-page layout.

d. Focus on Content

The design is minimalist, prioritizing the depth and quality of the content (the engineering projects, entrepreneurial ventures, and coaching history) over complex animations or heavily stylized graphics.
